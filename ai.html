How Artificial Intelligence Is Changing Technology

Artificial Intelligence, also known as AI, is a field of computer science focused on creating systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence. These tasks include understanding language, recognizing images, solving problems, analyzing information, making predictions, and learning from data.

Artificial intelligence is now used in healthcare, education, transportation, finance, robotics, cybersecurity, and software development. Modern AI systems can process large amounts of information quickly and assist people with complex tasks. As technology continues to develop, artificial intelligence is becoming an increasingly important part of modern computing and everyday life.
