Artificial Intelligence

How intelligent systems are changing the way we build, work and interact with technology.

Artificial Intelligence, also known as AI, is a field of computer science focused on developing systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence. These tasks include understanding natural language, recognizing images, solving problems, making predictions and learning patterns from large amounts of data.

Artificial intelligence is now used across healthcare, education, transportation, finance, robotics, cybersecurity and software development. Modern AI systems can process information at significant scale and assist people with complex tasks. As these technologies continue to develop, AI is becoming an increasingly important part of modern computing and digital products.
